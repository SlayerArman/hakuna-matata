# HAKUNA MATATA
> A 2D action platformer where you fight your way past zombies and traps to reach the end of the level.

## What to Do
Defeat zombies using your pistol or a close-range melee attack.
Watch out for Reflectors—if you shoot them, your bullet bounces straight back at you.
Stay alive and make it to the end of the map.

## Controls

Move Left / Right: A / D or Left / Right Arrow
Jump: Space
Crouch: S or Down Arrow
Aim Up: W or Up Arrow
Shoot: Left Click
Melee Attack: Right Click
Reload: R
Throw Grenade: F

## How the Game Ends
Find the Statue at the end of the level, walk up to it, and crouch in front of it to win the game.
